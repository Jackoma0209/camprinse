# UK Compliance Checklist

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice.

## Product Claims

- Do not claim water heating unless supplier documentation confirms it.
- Do not claim waterproofing unless a rating is documented.
- Do not claim pressure, flow rate or battery life unless documented.
- Do not claim certifications unless documents are held.
- Keep product description aligned with supplier evidence.

## Consumer Information

- Show business identity and contact details before launch.
- Provide clear delivery information.
- Provide clear returns and cancellation information.
- Provide total checkout cost before payment.
- Keep privacy and cookie information aligned with active providers.

## Product Safety

- Hold supplier manual and safety documentation.
- Confirm UK labelling requirements.
- Confirm battery and charging safety wording.
- Confirm importer or responsible-person requirements if applicable.
- Keep records of supplier documents and customer safety reports.

## Marketing

- Do not fake reviews, ratings or customer photos.
- Do not imply medical, drinking-water, pressure-washer or heated-shower use.
- Keep ads consistent with the landing-page claims.

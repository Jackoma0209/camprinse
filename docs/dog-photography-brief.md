# CampRinse Dog Photography Brief (Internal)

Internal filename map and drop-in instructions.  
**For freelancers:** send `docs/freelancer-photo-shoot-brief.md` instead (full shoot brief).

## Required files (priority order)

| Priority | Filename | Used on | Shot |
|----------|----------|---------|------|
| P0 | `hero-dog-boot.jpg` | Hero (right column) | Dog at open car boot / rear door after a muddy walk. Ideally mid-rinse or clearly about to rinse paws. UK outdoor light. |
| P0 | `before-muddy-paws.jpg` | Before / after | Close or mid shot: muddy paws, dirty boot liner or door sill, wet dog ready to jump in. |
| P0 | `after-clean-paws.jpg` | Before / after | Clean paws + kit visible at the car. Dog calm, car interior clean. Same dog/location as “before” if possible. |
| P1 | `product-kit.jpg` | Product section | Product flat-lay or boot-packed kit. Clean cream/green outdoor aesthetic. |

## Creative direction

- **Emotion:** relief after the walk — mess stays outside.
- **Hero message:** *Mud Stays Outside*
- **Setting:** UK car parks, trails, fields after rain — not studio-perfect.
- **People:** optional hands holding hose / dog; faces not required.
- **No fake reviews:** lifestyle photos only; no invented star ratings or quote cards.

## Technical specs

- Format: JPG (or convert to `.jpg` before adding)
- Hero: landscape ~1600×1200 or larger, sharp on mobile crop
- Before/after: landscape ~1400×1050
- Colour: natural outdoor; avoid heavy filters
- Rights: commercial use for CampRinse website and ads

## Optional extras (later ads)

- Owner kneeling rinsing a spaniel / lab / cockapoo at boot
- Van-life / multi-dog family walk ending
- 9:16 vertical clip for Meta / TikTok (15–30s): walk end → rinse → clean dog in car

## Drop-in steps

1. Export files with exact names above.
2. Copy into `public/images/`.
3. Commit and push to `main` (or redeploy).
4. Confirm hero / before-after / product show real photos on production.

Code: `lib/assets.ts` (auto-detects files at build time).

# CampRinse Freelancer Photo Shoot Brief

**Send this document as-is to a photographer, UGC creator, or lifestyle shooter.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Brand** | CampRinse |
| **Product** | Portable dog rinse kit (rechargeable outdoor rinse pump) |
| **Tagline** | Mud Stays Outside |
| **Audience** | UK dog owners who hate muddy cars, hallways and sofas |
| **Primary use** | Website hero + before/after + product shot (also usable in Meta/TikTok ads) |
| **Tone** | Premium outdoor, real, muddy, useful — not studio-glossy pet portraits |

---

## 1. Creative concept

**Story in one line:**  
The walk was great. The mess almost ruined the car and house — until CampRinse rinsed the paws at the boot.

**Emotion to capture:** relief + control after a muddy walk.

**Do**
- UK outdoor light (overcast is fine — often better)
- Real mud, wet fur, car boots, trail-end energy
- Product visible in “after” / rinse shots
- Natural colour; light grade only

**Don’t**
- Fake studio mud or stock-looking perfection
- Heavy filters, neon grades, AI-looking polish
- Invented review cards, star ratings, or quote overlays
- Dirty product packaging / messy branding text in frame
- Faces of children; adult faces optional (hands + dog preferred if model release is limited)

---

## 2. Must-deliver stills (P0)

Deliver **at least** these four final files (you may shoot many frames; we will pick).

| # | Final filename | Orientation | Framing | Exact shot description |
|---|----------------|-------------|---------|------------------------|
| 1 | `hero-dog-boot.jpg` | Landscape | Medium–wide, room for mobile crop | Open car boot or rear door. Muddy (or recently muddy) dog at the car after a walk. Ideal: owner/hands rinsing paws with hose/kit **or** clear “about to rinse” moment. Product or water bottle visible if natural. Background: car park / field edge / trail — not busy signage. |
| 2 | `before-muddy-paws.jpg` | Landscape | Close–medium | **The problem.** Muddy paws, dirty boot liner or door sill, wet dog ready to jump in. Messy and relatable. Product not required. |
| 3 | `after-clean-paws.jpg` | Landscape | Close–medium | **The solution.** Clean(er) paws; CampRinse kit visible at the boot; dog calm; car interior looking protected. Prefer **same dog + same location** as “before”. |
| 4 | `product-kit.jpg` | Landscape or slight top-down | Product hero | Kit laid out cleanly **or** packed in boot with lead/towel. Cream/green outdoor palette. No competing brand logos. |

### Nice-to-have stills (P1)
- Hands only, rinsing one muddy paw close-up  
- Kit in boot next to muddy wellies  
- Multi-dog or family walk ending (if models available)  
- Van / estate car lifestyle variant

| Spec | Requirement |

## 5. Talent & location checklist

**Dogs (prefer 1–2):**
- Medium/large breeds common in UK walks (spaniel, lab, cockapoo, collie, etc.)
- Comfortable with water around paws and car boot noise  
- Ideally already muddy from a real walk (or wet ground)

**Location ideas:**
- Country park car park after rain  
- Coastal path + car park (sand + mud variants)  
- Field gateway / trail head with room to shoot safely  

**Props:**
- CampRinse product unit (client provides)  
- Clean water bottle / container  
- Old towel, lead, wellies (optional)  
- Car with boot that opens cleanly in frame  

**Safety:**
- Public land / permission as needed  
- No dogs left unattended; no road risk for people or animals  
- Clean water only in kit during shoot

## 7. Shot list for the day (run order)

1. **Walk first** — get dog naturally muddy/wet (15–30 min).  
2. **Before sequence** at car: muddy paws, dirty boot, “about to jump in”.  
3. **Rinse sequence**: set up kit, water container, rinse paws (hero + detail).  
4. **After sequence**: clean paws, calm dog, clean interior impression.  
5. **Product beauty**: flat-lay / boot pack.  
6. **Vertical video** pass of the full story (2–3 takes).  

Estimate: **2–3 hours** on location + edit time.
